Sterling is not its own municipality.

Sterling does not have its own city hall, mayor or separate municipal government. It is an unincorporated community within Loudoun County, a separate county from Fairfax's own Fairfax County, so local services and governance run through Loudoun County rather than a Sterling-specific town office. That does not change how we cover the area: the 20164 and 20165 ZIPs are inside our service range.

We are based near Fairfax, not in Sterling itself, so there is no local office or standing crew stationed here. What we offer is a phone line that answers calls from these ZIPs and technicians who route out to handle them.

What we handle on calls from Sterling.

Sterling's residential streets combine established subdivisions with newer development near the area's commercial corridors, and that mix shapes the calls we get. Newer construction can still have unsealed utility penetrations left from the build process, while established homes commonly show settled gaps at sills and rooflines.

Indoors, calls commonly describe mice moving into a garage or crawl space as the weather turns cold, or a smell suggesting a dead rodent in a wall. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for that indoor pattern, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity.
