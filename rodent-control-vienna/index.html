Vienna is its own incorporated town.

Vienna has its own town charter, its own local government and its own town council, which sets it apart from many of the county's other named communities that do not have that structure. That distinction does not change how we cover the area: the 22181 ZIP is inside our service range, and calls from Vienna get the same response as any other town on our list.

We are based near Fairfax, not in Vienna itself, so there is no local office or standing crew stationed here. What we offer is a phone line that answers calls from this ZIP and technicians who route out to handle them.

What we handle on calls from Vienna.

Vienna's housing includes a mix of older, established streets and newer infill construction, and both styles create their own entry points for mice and rats. Older homes tend to have settled gaps at sills and rooflines, while newer construction can still have unsealed utility penetrations from the build process.

Indoors, calls commonly describe mice moving into a garage or crawl space as the weather turns cold, or a smell suggesting a dead rodent in a wall. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for that indoor pattern, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity along fence lines and yards.

Established streets and settled construction.

Many Vienna properties sit on streets developed decades ago, and homes of that age often show the same kind of settled gaps at sills and chimneys that we check on older homes throughout our coverage area. Our rodent exclusion service covers sealing these gaps with materials that hold up over time, rather than a quick foam fix that fails within a season.

Yards, sheds and outdoor activity.

Vienna properties with larger yards or a detached shed need the same outdoor attention as anywhere else in our coverage area, since burrows and outdoor nests can support activity separate from what is happening inside the house. Our backyard and outdoor rodent control service treats these outdoor areas across the full extent of a lot.

Scheduling a visit in Vienna.

Because Vienna has its own town government, some residents assume that changes how a rodent visit gets scheduled, but it does not. We coordinate directly with you by phone, the same as any other address in our coverage area, and no separate permission from the town itself is needed for a technician to visit a private residence. If your property is close to a town-maintained park or right-of-way, mentioning that helps us plan around any nearby landscaping crews or maintenance schedules, but it is not a requirement for booking a visit.

When to call it an emergency.

Active chewing near wiring, a nest close to a heat source, or an animal loose inside living space are reasons to flag a call as urgent. The line is open 24/7, and we prioritize these situations against the regular schedule rather than making you wait for the next open slot.

Vienna's short distance from our Fairfax base means travel time is rarely a factor in how quickly a technician can reach your address, and same-day scheduling is realistic on most days the line has room.

Before we arrive.

  • Keep pets and children away from any suspected nest or burrow.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings.
  • Do not set out loose bait or poison before we arrive.
  • Note where and when you saw or heard activity.
  • Clear access to the crawl space, garage or basement if you can do so safely.
