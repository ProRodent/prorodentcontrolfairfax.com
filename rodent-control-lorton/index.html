That does not change how we cover the area: the 22079 ZIP is inside our service range, and calls from Lorton get the same response as any incorporated city on our list.

What we handle on calls from Lorton.

Lorton sits near the Occoquan River and includes a mix of residential subdivisions and areas with more open, less developed land than closer-in parts of our coverage area.
