Falls Church is an independent city.

Falls Church, like Fairfax itself, is an independent city under Virginia law, with its own municipal government rather than being part of a surrounding county. It is one of the smallest independent cities in the state, and its actual boundary is compact, surrounded closely by unincorporated Fairfax and Arlington County land.

Because of a known quirk in how postal ZIP codes are drawn in this area, some ZIPs commonly associated with Falls Church, including 22041, 22044 and 22046, extend into unincorporated county land that sits outside the small independent city's actual boundary. We frame our coverage honestly as the Falls Church area rather than claiming every address in these ZIPs sits inside the city limits, and we treat 22042, the closest and most central ZIP, as the best-supported case for the independent city itself.

What we handle on calls from the Falls Church area.

The Falls Church area combines a compact, walkable core with surrounding residential streets that have a mix of older and newer construction. Older homes near the city center commonly show settled gaps at sills and rooflines, while newer construction can still have unsealed utility penetrations.

Indoors, calls commonly describe mice moving into a garage or crawl space as the weather turns cold, or a smell suggesting a dead rodent in a wall. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for that indoor pattern, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity.
