Arlington is a county, not a town or city.

Arlington is unique in Virginia: it is a fully urbanized county with no incorporated towns inside it, unlike most of the county names that appear elsewhere on our coverage list, which describe unincorporated communities within a larger, more rural county. There is no separate Arlington city government, since Arlington County's own county government serves this role directly. We frame this page honestly as Arlington County rather than describing it as an ordinary town.

Arlington has many ZIP codes across a dense, urbanized area, so we do not attempt to list every one here. We cover addresses including 22201, 22204, 22205, 22206 and 22207, among others closer to our service range.

What we handle on calls from Arlington.

Arlington's dense mix of high-rise buildings, older rowhouses and single-family neighborhoods creates a wide range of entry-point patterns. Older single-family homes commonly show settled gaps at sills and rooflines, while multi-unit buildings can see rodent issues that originate in a shared wall, utility chase or basement common area.

Indoors, calls commonly describe mice moving into a garage, basement or crawl space as the weather turns cold. Our mouse control service covers the trap-and-seal approach for that indoor pattern, and rat control addresses outdoor, ground-level activity common near urban green space and dense residential blocks.
